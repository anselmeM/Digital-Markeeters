Vogue Essence

Redefining editorial luxury through an immersive, typographically-driven digital experience.

Scroll to explore arrow_downward

Project Brief

Vogue Essence approached Marcy Studios with a bold vision: to create a digital platform that mirrors the drama and intimacy of high-fashion editorial. The challenge was translating the tactile luxury of print into a web experience without sacrificing speed or accessibility.

We embarked on a journey to design a UI that feels weightless yet authoritative. By leveraging Bodoni Moda's editorial gravitas, a tightly constrained warm palette, and expansive negative space, we crafted an interface that acts as a quiet frame for their striking photography.

The resulting platform not only showcases their seasonal collections but serves as a living manifesto of their house identity — ensuring every scroll feels deliberate, every transition intentional.
